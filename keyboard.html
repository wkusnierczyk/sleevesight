Type on a computer

Type here, and the text appears in a field of Sleevesight on your phone: an API key, for instance.

This browser cannot use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer with Bluetooth.

  1. On the phone, open the field’s dialog, tap the keyboard icon in its field (Type on a computer), and scan this code.
  2. When the phone says it is waiting, choose Connect here and pick the phone: it is the only device listed. It shows as Unknown or Unsupported Device, since the phone does not advertise its name, unless Share phone’s name with Type on a computer is on in the app’s settings, under Secrets; then it shows by its Bluetooth name.

  3. Type. The phone’s field follows what you type; save it on the phone.

    Too long: the phone takes up to 480 bytes (480 letters of English, fewer of other scripts). Shorten the text to send it.

Not connected.

What you type goes from your computer to your phone alone, over Bluetooth, encrypted; nothing goes through a server. The code holds a key made for this visit alone; the phone forgets it when you close its dialog. Reload the page for a new one.

While the phone waits for the computer, it advertises over Bluetooth, and devices nearby that scan for Bluetooth can see that advertisement: a random identifier made for this visit, and, only with Share phone’s name with Type on a computer on, the phone’s Bluetooth name.
