What's new in 0.1.9:
- Load keys: the phone shows five words to type on the computer, which seal the QR code of your keys: a photo of the code alone gives nothing away.
- A key scanned from a QR code is never shown, not even in its own dialog.
- Before Android's notification access warning, the app says plainly what it reads: never your notifications.
- When an insight's reply breaks off or comes back empty, the app says how; no more empty cards.
- Covers are fetched over HTTPS only.
