What's new in 0.1.2:
- "What leaves the phone" now matches the privacy policy, and lists all the app reads from your player.
- Settings say why web search cannot run, and when the saved Last.fm key can no longer be read.
- Diagnostics: an export cut short now ends as failed, so a new one can start.
- Screen readers announce each citation and can jump to its source line.
- Sources: the chips sit level, and lines start in one column.
- Updated for Android 17 (still runs on Android 8 and later).
