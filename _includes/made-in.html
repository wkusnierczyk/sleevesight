{% comment %} Where Sleevesight is made and how to support it (#839): the one source of the line, which every page of the site that shows it includes. The documentation pages (`_layouts/docs.html`) and the pages with the default layout (`_layouts/default.html`: the privacy policy and the design notes) show it under the page's title, the README page (`readme-page.html`) under the README's tagline, and the product page in its footer (`site-footer.html` with `made_in=true`). Its look is each layout's, as a byline: smaller and muted. The flag is drawn rather than an emoji, which some systems show as the letters "CH": the Swiss flag in its official proportions (a square of 32, the cross's arms 6 wide and 7 long), as high as a capital letter of the site's fonts (0.71 em), on the baseline; hidden from screen readers, since the text names the country. The donate link is the platform in `.github/FUNDING.yml`; the charity link is swissdonations.ch's list of Swiss charities (#1156), for those who would rather give to a good cause. {% endcomment -%}
