Who made a page is set by hand in its front matter, `provenance`: `human-wrote` or `ai-wrote`, and optionally the other side's `-edited` or `-reviewed`, e.g. `provenance: [ai-wrote, human-reviewed]`. Writing comes first, so a page has one *wrote*, shown first whatever the order in the list; the first `-edited` or `-reviewed` in the list shows as the second label when it is the other side's, and otherwise there is none. That leaves six combinations. A page without the key, or without a *wrote*, shows no labels. Each label links to the Process page's *Lessons learned*, in its who's colour, and ends in a full stop hidden on screen, so a screen reader reads "AI wrote." and "Human reviewed." apart. {% endcomment %}
