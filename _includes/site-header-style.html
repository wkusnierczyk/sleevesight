{% comment %} The header of the product page (`index.html`) and of the documentation pages (`_layouts/docs.html`, #501): the stacked logo with the menu beside it, large at the top and small scrolled down; the intro that plays before it; and the footer. Each page sets the colours these use: `--bg`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--sight`, `--f-ui`, and the logo's `--tile-bg`, `--tile-edge`, `--tile-ink`, `--rim`, `--core` and `--groove`; a page with a dark mode also sets the footer's `--prov-human` and `--prov-ai` in it. The header's markup is `site-header.html`, its scripts `site-intro-head.html` (in the head) and `site-header-script.html` (at the end of the body), and the footer `site-footer.html`. The rules are in `site-header-style.css`, inlined here and served as a file in `assets/site/chrome.css`, for a page whose policy admits no inline style (`api-keys.html`, #764). {% endcomment %}
