materialkolor, by Jordon de Hoog (https://github.com/jordond/materialkolor)

Sleevesight takes the now-playing screen's colours from the album art with materialkolor's
material-color-utilities (com.materialkolor:material-color-utilities), a Kotlin port of Google's
Material Color Utilities (https://github.com/material-foundation/material-color-utilities).
