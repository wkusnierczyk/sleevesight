# Documentation

Sleevesight is an Android app that shows short, sourced insights about the song you are playing. The [product page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) says what it does; these pages say how to use it, how it works and how it is made.

## Using the app

- **[A typical journey]({{ '/docs/journey/' | relative_url }})**: from installing the app to using it day to day, step by step: what each screen asks you to decide, and what follows from it.
- **[Usage]({{ '/docs/tutorials/usage/' | relative_url }})**: each part of the app in detail: the home screen, the insight and its citations, the sources, *Write insight* and *Rewrite*, the history, the players, and what happens offline, without a key or when a source fails.
- **[Settings]({{ '/docs/tutorials/settings/' | relative_url }})**: every setting: what it does, its default and why, and what changing it sends or keeps.
- **[Privacy policy]({{ '/privacy.html' | relative_url }})**: what leaves the phone, to whom, and where Sleevesight stands under the GDPR and the EU AI Act.

## How it works

- **[Architecture]({{ '/docs/architecture/' | relative_url }})**: the app's parts, what talks to what, and what leaves the phone for which service.
- **[Dependencies]({{ '/docs/dependencies/' | relative_url }})**: the libraries the app ships, what each does in it, its licence and limits; and the data sources and fonts.
- **[Solutions]({{ '/docs/solutions/' | relative_url }})**: the problems Sleevesight solves in ways a reader would not guess, invented here or reimplemented on purpose.

## How it is made

- **[Process]({{ '/docs/process/' | relative_url }})**: how the app is built, reviewed, tested and released.
- **[Testing Sleevesight]({{ '/docs/tester/alpha/' | relative_url }})**: the brief for testers in the closed test on Google Play.

The design documents behind these pages are in the repository, under [`design/`](https://github.com/wkusnierczyk/sleevesight/tree/main/design).
