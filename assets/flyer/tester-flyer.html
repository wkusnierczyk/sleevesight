Testing Sleevesight

Five steps, then 14 days opted in. Thank you for testing.

  1. 1

    Opt in and install

    Scan the code and accept, signed in to the Google account you were invited on, the one your phone's Play Store uses. Then install Sleevesight from Google Play.

    The Play Store says Item not found? Open play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.sleevesight in a browser, with the same account, and tap Install.

    Opt in on Google Play
  2. 2

    Turn on notification access

    On the first screen, tap Open notification access, turn on Sleevesight, then come back and tap Continue. It lets the app see what your player is playing; it does not read your messages or other notifications.

  3. 3

    Scan your key

    Open Settings with the cogwheel at the top of the home screen, unfold Secrets, tap Load keys, then Next under its five words (or read them to us, if we make your code with you), point the camera at the code we gave you, and tap Save. Then, under AI services, switch on Anthropic.

    The key is yours alone: please do not share it. Using your own key instead is fine too.

  4. 4

    Play a song, tap Write insight

    Play music in Spotify, then open Sleevesight. Your first tap on Write insight writes the insight; after it, a new one follows each track change.

  5. 5

    Something odd? The bug button, then Report

    Tap the bug at the bottom right of the home screen as soon as you notice it. The report takes the moment for you: the track, the insight, what the app did. You see all of it before you send it from your mail app.

Worth trying

  • Skip a track in your player, and watch the insight follow.
  • Tap a citation chip at the end of a sentence to see its source.
  • Follow a second player: switch it on under Players in Settings once it has played music.

Stay opted in for 14 days, and keep the app installed, even on days you do not use it.
