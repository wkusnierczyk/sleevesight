Follow-up example: labels or type

The product page's Deep dive example, three ways (#224). A is the page as it is. B and C drop the speaker labels and tell the turns apart by type alone: the question upright, the answer in italics with its byline under it. Italics already mark the album title, so B sets it upright inside the italic answer, and C puts it in quotation marks. Whichever is chosen, the in-app follow-up view (#214) follows it.

A. Labels (current)

Explicit; a screen reader reads "You" and "AI" before each turn.

Deep dive

Paul McCartney

You Tell me more about his career.

AI He joined Lennon's skiffle group, the Quarrymen, in 1957, which became the Beatles in 1960., source W1 After the band split, he released his first solo album, McCartney, in 1970, and formed Wings with his wife Linda and Denny Laine., source W2 His songwriting partnership with Lennon remains the most successful in music history., source W3 by Claude Haiku 5.5

B. Type, titles upright

Quieter; the title reverses to upright inside the italics.

Deep dive

Paul McCartney

You asked: Tell me more about his career.

The AI answered: He joined Lennon's skiffle group, the Quarrymen, in 1957, which became the Beatles in 1960., source W1 After the band split, he released his first solo album, McCartney, in 1970, and formed Wings with his wife Linda and Denny Laine., source W2 His songwriting partnership with Lennon remains the most successful in music history., source W3 by Claude Haiku 5.5

Quieter; the title in quotation marks, as a song's would be.
