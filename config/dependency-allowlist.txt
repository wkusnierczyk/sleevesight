# Groups allowed on the shipped runtime classpath of any module, with their licence.
# An entry matches its group exactly; `group.*` also matches its subgroups, and `group:name`
# matches that one module only. Prefer exact groups: `androidx.*` would admit
# androidx.privacysandbox.ads, an ads API.
# Adding a line is a reviewed decision: no analytics, crash-reporting or ads SDKs
# (design, Structural constraints), and every licence GPL-3.0 compatible.
#
# When the Phase 0 probe was removed (#30), only `org.jetbrains.kotlin` and `org.jetbrains`
# were in use; the rest, `com.google.guava` and `org.jspecify` included, were sized to the
# probe. When `:app` came (#47), it used all of them again, and the Compose groups and further
# AndroidX groups were added, most of them pulled in by Compose UI.
# The move to Compose 1.12 and Navigation 2.10 (#146) took some of those groups off the
# classpath, and their lines went with them.
#
